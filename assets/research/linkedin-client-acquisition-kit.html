Daily Workflow (30 min/day)

Setup required (one-time): Install Claude Chrome extension. Open LinkedIn in Chrome. Claude can now read profiles, draft DMs, and research prospects directly.
Daily Checklist
  • Morning (10 min): Comment on 3 posts from prospects or SEO influencers. Add genuine insight, not "great post." Aim for 2+ sentences with a specific takeaway.
  • Midday (10 min): Send 5 personalized DMs to prospects (use templates below). Have Claude scan each prospect's profile first for personalization hooks.
  • Afternoon (10 min): Reply to every comment on your posts within 30 min of posting. Reply to every DM response. Flag hot leads for follow-up.
Weekly Cadence
  • Monday: Publish post (educational — SEO data insight)

  • Wednesday: Publish post (case study / result)

  • Friday: Publish post (personal story / lesson learned)

Finding Prospects

Who to target on LinkedIn

Your ICP: small business owners spending $0–2k/mo on SEO who need professional-grade work but can't afford $10k+ agency retainers. Look for:

  • Business owners posting about marketing struggles or asking SEO questions
  • People engaging with SEO content (liking/commenting on posts from Ahrefs, Moz, Search Engine Journal)
  • Small business owners who recently launched or redesigned a website
  • E-commerce owners, local service businesses, SaaS founders with <50 employees
  • People posting in Skool communities or LinkedIn groups about growth
Claude Chrome workflow: Open a prospect's profile → ask Claude to summarize their business, recent posts, and identify a personalization hook → use one of the DM templates below with that hook inserted.

DM Templates (Personalize Before Sending)

Rule: Every DM must reference something specific about the prospect. Never send a template verbatim. Use Claude Chrome to scan their profile first and fill in the [brackets].
Template 1 — After they engage with SEO content
Hey [Name] — saw you commented on [person]'s post about [topic]. Curious, is [their company] actively working on SEO right now or is it more on the back burner?
Why it works: References their real activity. Asks an open-ended question. No pitch.
Template 2 — Cold outreach to a business owner
Hey [Name] — I was looking at [their website] and noticed [specific observation: e.g., "your service pages aren't showing up for any keywords in Ahrefs"]. Not pitching anything, just thought it was worth flagging since [reason: e.g., "those pages look like they could rank with some small fixes"]. Happy to share what I found if you're interested.
Why it works: Leads with value. Specific observation proves you did homework. "Not pitching" lowers guard.
Template 3 — Warm follow-up (they accepted connection)
Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I noticed you're running [business type] — how's the organic traffic side going for you? I work with small businesses on SEO and always curious what's working (or not) for others in [their industry].
Why it works: Acknowledges the connection. Shows genuine curiosity. Opens conversation without asking for a meeting.
Template 4 — Pattern interrupt (voice note script)
"Hey [Name], this is Shane from PropeloSEO. I was checking out [their website] and honestly, I think you're leaving some traffic on the table with [specific thing]. Didn't want to type out a novel — just thought I'd say hey and see if you'd want to hear what I found. No pressure either way."
Why it works: Voice notes have 3× open rate of text DMs. Casual, not scripted-sounding. Send as LinkedIn voice message.
Template 5 — Referral ask to existing connection
Hey [Name] — quick question. Do you know any small business owners who've been frustrated with their SEO or getting burned by an agency? I help businesses get agency-grade SEO work for $97/mo and I'm looking to bring on a few more this month. Would mean a lot if anyone comes to mind.
Why it works: People love making introductions. Specific ask ("small business owners frustrated with SEO") makes it easy to think of someone.
