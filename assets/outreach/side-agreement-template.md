**Revenue Share Agreement — Affiliate Promotion**

**Between:** Shane Hellmrich / Propelo SEO L.L.C. ("Shane") and {{partner_name}} ("Partner")
**Date:** {{date}}

1. **What this covers.** Partner will promote Shane's affiliate link for AI SEO Rainmakers ({{link}}) to Partner's audience. Shane will supply review content, comparison pages, swipe copy and cross-promotion to Shane's own community.

2. **How commissions work.** Skool pays member-affiliate commissions only to the link holder (Shane). Skool cannot split commissions natively. Shane will therefore pay Partner directly under this agreement.

3. **Split.** Partner receives {{X}}% of the net commission Skool pays Shane for each member who joins via the link during the term and who Partner can reasonably be shown to have referred (agreed method: {{UTM / dedicated landing page / coupon-style attribution}}).

4. **Net means net.** Commissions reversed by refund, chargeback or negative balance are deducted from the following payout. Neither party owes the other for reversals beyond amounts already paid.

5. **Reporting.** Shane sends Partner a screenshot of the Skool affiliate dashboard and a simple reconciliation on the {{1st}} of each month.

6. **Payment.** Within {{7}} days of the report, by {{Wise / PayPal / bank}}, in USD.

7. **Conduct.** No fake urgency beyond the real founding-price scarcity, no invented testimonials, no paid ads on the "Skool" keyword (prohibited by Skool), no spam. Either party can require removal of copy that breaches this.

8. **Term and exit.** Rolling monthly. Either party may end it with 7 days' notice. Commissions on members already attributed continue to be shared for {{6}} months after exit, then stop.

9. **Not a partnership.** Independent contractors. Each handles their own taxes.

10. **Disputes.** Talk first. If unresolved in 30 days, either party may walk with no further obligation beyond amounts already earned.
