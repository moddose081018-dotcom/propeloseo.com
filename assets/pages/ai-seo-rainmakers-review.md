# AI SEO Rainmakers Review: What $289/Month Actually Buys You

*Disclosure: I'm a paying member and an affiliate. If you join through my link I earn a commission. It doesn't change your price.*

I joined AI SEO Rainmakers in August 2026. This is what it is, what it costs, and who shouldn't bother.

## The short version

Rainmakers is Charles Floate's private Skool community for AI-era SEO. It costs $289/month. The founding block locks that price for life; after it sells out the price moves to $299+. At the time of writing it has 351 members and a 5.0 rating from 31 reviews.

The core product is not the courses. It's the weekly live calls where Floate answers every question on screen and tears down member sites in real time. If you'd use that, it's worth the money. If you wouldn't, it isn't.

## What's inside

- **Weekly live 1-on-1 support calls.** Screen up, live Q&A. Floate stays until every question is answered.
- **Every test with full methodology.** Not "this works" — the setup, the variables, the result.
- **Tools, repos, site audits and CharlesGPT 3.1.** His personal stack, downloadable.
- **New courses and intel monthly.**
- **Dark Library and AI SEO framework bonuses.**
- **He answers every question inside the community**, not just on calls.

## Who it's for

- Agency owners and freelancers already billing for SEO who need a weekly feedback loop, not another course.
- Affiliates and site operators in competitive or grey niches where the standard playbook is too slow.
- Anyone who has a site to bring to a teardown. That's where the value concentrates.

## Who it's NOT for

- Beginners. There's no hand-holding track. If you can't already build and rank a site, you'll be lost on the calls.
- Anyone who wants pure white-hat content. Members describe the material as advanced and, in places, black-hat-adjacent. Know what you're buying.
- People who won't attend calls. Passive members are paying $289/month for a course library — that's poor value.

## The refund

7-day money-back if you DM before reaching Level 3. That's enough time for one or two live calls. Use them, then decide.
