Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At PropeloSEO ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit propeloseo.com or use our services.

Summary: We collect minimal personal information, never sell your data, and use industry-standard security for all client data and SEO analytics.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide: When you contact us, subscribe to the Growth Membership, purchase an AI Visibility Audit, or apply for the founding cohort, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Website URL and business information
  • Payment information (processed securely via Stripe)
  • Website access credentials you share for SEO work

Automatically collected information: When you visit our website, we may collect standard technical data including IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and referring URLs.

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver your Growth Packs and membership services
  • To conduct technical crawls, keyword tracking, and AI visibility monitoring on your behalf
  • To communicate about your membership and deliverables
  • To process payments via Stripe
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. SEO data and website access

As part of the Growth Membership, we access your website using credentials you provide (such as Google Search Console, analytics, or CMS access). This access is used solely to deliver your agreed services. We do not share your website data or SEO analytics with other clients or third parties.

4. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to deliver your membership:

  • Stripe for payment processing
  • Ahrefs for keyword and backlink analysis
  • DataForSEO for SERP tracking and AI visibility monitoring
  • Screaming Frog for technical crawling

Each service has its own privacy policy governing their handling of data.

5. Cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use our website. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information and website access credentials. Payment information is handled entirely by Stripe and never stored on our servers.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at shanehellmrich@proton.me.

8. Data retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your membership is active and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal obligations. SEO data and analytics generated during your membership are yours; we can provide an export on request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via email to active members.

10. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email us at shanehellmrich@proton.me.
