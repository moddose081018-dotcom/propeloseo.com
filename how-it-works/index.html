How It Works

The Growth Membership runs as a 90-Day Visibility Sprint. Three months of measurable progress, then keep going on the same terms.

The 90-Day Visibility Sprint

Every membership begins with a structured three-month sprint. Each month delivers a specific outcome, building on the last. After the sprint closes, your membership continues on the same terms — same price, same monthly Growth Pack, same data sources.

Month 1

Baseline + Quick-Win Pack

We set up your measurement baseline and deliver a real, implemented fix within five business days.

  • Full technical crawl (up to 500 URLs)
  • Keyword and SERP baseline (up to 50 keywords)
  • Competitor and backlink-gap snapshot
  • Limited AI Visibility Baseline
  • Three opportunities ranked by commercial value
  • One eligible low-risk fix, implemented with your approval
Month 2

Biggest Opportunity Pack

With the baseline set, your second Growth Pack targets the highest-impact opportunity identified.

  • Updated monitoring and critical alerts
  • AI-engine visibility tracking
  • One publish-ready Growth Pack
  • One bankable Growth Credit issued
  • Plain-English progress record
Month 3

90-Day Progress Report

Your sprint closes with a clear evidence report. The membership then continues on the same terms.

  • Baseline vs. current comparison
  • Work delivered and implemented
  • Observed movement with honest attribution
  • One Growth Pack included
  • Bonus credit on sprint completion
  • Continue your membership or cancel freely

What happens after the sprint?

Your membership continues at the same $97/month rate. You keep receiving one Growth Pack per month, ongoing AI visibility monitoring, critical alerts, and bankable Growth Credits. Cancel anytime — no lock-in, no cancellation fees.

The principle: Every Growth Pack is built from live professional data, scoped to a single high-impact opportunity, and personally QA'd before delivery. We guarantee the deliverable, never the outcome.

What you need to provide

  • Website access: Read-only access to your site for the technical crawl.
  • Written approval: Before we implement any fix in month one, you sign off on the specific change.
  • Target keywords: Up to 50 keywords you want tracked. We'll help you choose if you're unsure.
  • Competitor list: Two to three competitors. Again, we'll suggest if needed.
