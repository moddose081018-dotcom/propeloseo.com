# Revenue Model — Rainmakers Creator Partnership Engine

Funnel math for the $20K/month goal, built on cited 2026 benchmarks. Three scenarios
(conservative / base / optimistic). Purpose: an honest read on whether $20K/mo in 6 months
is realistic, and which variable matters most.

**Bottom line up front:** $20K/mo from the Rainmakers line in 6 months requires the
**optimistic** end of every assumption. The **base case is ~$5–6K/mo at month 6**, reaching
$20K around **month 10–14** as recurring compounds. The target is achievable but the
6-month timeline is aggressive.

---

## Benchmarks used (sources at bottom)

| Input | Value used | Source basis |
|---|---|---|
| Cold outreach → interested reply | 8% / 15% / 25% | Small (<50) hyper-personalized lists average ~5.8% reply; we offer money with no ask, so interest runs higher than generic B2B (1.5–3%). |
| Interested → active promoting partner | ~50–80% follow-through | Judgment; many say yes, fewer actually post. |
| Members referred per active partner (6 mo) | 3 / 8 / 15 | High-ticket affiliate conversion is low (~0.5% of a matched audience); a bought-in partner promoting a $289/mo offer to an SEO/agency audience over months. |
| Refund loss (7-day window) | 7% | Rainmakers' 7-day money-back. |
| Monthly churn | 6% | Skool high-ticket ($200+/mo, active programming) runs 2–3% best case, 8–15% average; $289/mo with weekly calls → ~5–6%. |
| Commission per member | $144.50/mo | 50% of $289. |

**Target:** $20,000 ÷ $144.50 = **138 net active referred members.**

## Why recurring changes the timeline

Recurring revenue compounds: retained members stack under new adds. At **base** assumptions
(~8 gross adds/month, 6% churn), steady state ≈ 8 ÷ 0.06 ≈ **133 members ≈ $19K/mo** — reached
around **month 12–14**, not month 6. The 6-month figure is a ramp snapshot; the 12-month figure
is where the model actually delivers $20K at base assumptions.

## Budget / cost of acquisition

This is an **organic** engine — near-zero cash outlay, time-dominated.

- **Required cash:** $289/mo Rainmakers membership (needed to be a member-affiliate and to write an honest review). **Break-even = 2 referred members.** Each member repays the membership in ~2 months.
- **Outreach:** ~$0. Optional email-finder tool ~$50/mo. No paid ads on the "Skool" keyword (banned by Skool).
- **Real cost = time:** personalized outreach to 50 prospects, ongoing partner support, content upkeep. If a VA/time is valued, that's the dominant "budget" line, not cash.
- **Effective CAC:** near-zero cash; the constraint is partner activation and their conversion, not spend.

---

## The three variables that decide it (ranked by leverage)

1. **Members referred per partner** — highest leverage, highest uncertainty. Everything hinges on partners actually converting their audience to a $289/mo offer. Moving this from 3 → 8 → 15 is the difference between $1K and $17K/mo.
2. **Number of genuinely active partners** — need 6+ bought-in promoters, not just sign-ups. A partner who joins but never posts contributes ~0.
3. **Churn** — at 6%/mo you lose ~30% of the base over 6 months. Recurring only compounds if monthly adds exceed churn.

## Honest verdict

- **$20K/mo in 6 months:** optimistic-case only — every assumption at its top end. Not the expected outcome.
- **$20K/mo in ~12 months:** realistic at base assumptions, driven by recurring compounding.
- **Fastest lever:** don't chase more prospects — deepen partner activation and per-partner conversion. 6 great partners beat 20 lukewarm ones.
- **De-risker:** the full 4-line stack (especially Citation Playbook, which Shane keeps 100% of) matters — supply its price + member count to model line 3.
