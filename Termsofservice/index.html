1. Growth Membership

Subscription terms

The Growth Membership is billed at $97 per month via Stripe. There is no minimum commitment. You may cancel at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

What is included

Each month of active membership includes:

  • Updated technical, ranking, competitor and AI-engine visibility monitoring with critical alerts

  • One Growth Credit (subject to the credit terms below)
  • A plain-English progress record
  • Member pricing on additional Growth Packs

Service limits

The Growth Membership covers one website, a crawl of up to 500 URLs, tracking of up to 50 keywords, a comparison against up to three competitors, one content swap per pack, and one revision round per pack.

Welcome phase (month one)

Your first month includes a full technical and search baseline within the limits above, a limited AI Visibility Baseline (a defined subset of buyer questions and engines, deliberately narrower than the standalone AI Visibility Audit), three ranked opportunities, and your first Quick-Win Growth Pack with one eligible fix implemented.

2. Quick-Win Pack and eligible scope

The first Growth Pack delivered to new members is a Quick-Win Pack. It includes one eligible low-risk fix, implemented with your written approval, within five business days of completed onboarding, required access and written approval.

Eligible fixes

  • Metadata changes (title tags, meta descriptions)
  • Broken internal link repairs
  • Basic schema corrections
  • Indexability-setting corrections
  • One small on-page improvement

Excluded from eligible scope

  • Development work
  • Template or theme changes
  • Site migrations
  • Complex schema implementations
  • Plugin conflicts
  • High-risk site-wide changes

From month two onward, Growth Packs are publish-ready deliverables. Routine implementation is excluded unless purchased separately.
