# Operating Model — Creator Partnerships ("Empty Shelf" method)

The governing model for the whole project. It adopts the **structure** from the reference video
(the "creator is the gold mine" / empty-shelf method) but the **thing on the shelf is the Skool
affiliate link — not a bespoke ebook.** No product creation, no Synthesize, no Whop.

## The core idea (kept from the video)
- **The gold mine is the creator, not the customer.** Mid-size creators (10K–100K) have an
  audience that trusts them but often nothing to sell — an "empty shelf."
- **Your job:** find an empty shelf and put one good thing on it.
- **Three steps:** (1) find one mid-size creator, (2) give them something their fans will buy,
  (3) put it on a platform that fulfils + splits the money automatically.
- **Faceless.** Systematic, tested, automated outreach — never generic "let's collab" DMs.

## What we KEEP
- Creator-as-goldmine framing and the empty-shelf pitch.
- Mid-size targeting (10K–100K followers, or platform equivalent).
- Systematic, automated outreach with A/B'd angles and auto follow-ups. **Our daily routine
  (`daily-routine.md`) is the ListKit-equivalent** — it drafts personalized outreach to `outbox/`.
- Faceless operation.
- Revenue split with the creator.

## What we CHANGE (per Shane, 2026-09-13)
- **No ebook. No bespoke product creation. No Synthesize, no Whop.** We do not build or sell our
  own digital products.
- **The "thing on the shelf" = the Skool affiliate link.**
  - **Primary offer:** AI SEO Rainmakers (Charles Floate, $289/mo → 50% recurring = $144.50/mo).
  - **Secondary offer:** Skool *platform* referral (40% of $99/mo = $39.60/mo) for creators who
    want to start their own community.
- **Fulfilment + split = Skool's native referral** (or a manual side-agreement for deal shape B).
  Not Whop.

## The honest constraint this creates
The video's method works on **any** niche because it sells a cheap (~$14) impulse product. We are
placing a **$289/mo high-ticket subscription** on the shelf. That only converts for audiences
already in the money/marketing world. So:

- **Niche is NOT "any creator."** A parenting or fitness audience will not buy a $289/mo AI-SEO
  community — pushing it there would just burn the relationship.
- **Target niche = SEO, digital marketing, agencies, "make money online," AI/automation,
  freelancing/solopreneur.** Broader than our current pure-SEO list, but bounded by what a
  high-ticket AI-SEO offer can actually sell.
- **The Skool platform affiliate is the one arm that CAN go broad** — helping any creator start
  their own community fits the empty-shelf idea for any niche. Treat it as the wide-niche play.

## Two offers, two shelves
| | Rainmakers affiliate | Skool platform affiliate |
|---|---|---|
| What the creator's audience gets | Access to a $289/mo AI-SEO community | (the creator starts their OWN community) |
| Who it fits | Creators with SEO / marketing / agency / MMO / AI audiences | Any creator who wants to monetize with a community |
| Your commission | $144.50/mo per member (50%) | $39.60/mo per creator (40% of $99) |
| Niche breadth | Constrained (money/marketing-adjacent) | Broad (any niche) |

## Three steps, our version
1. **Find** one mid-size creator (10K–100K) in the target niches with an engaged audience and no
   competing high-ticket offer.
2. **Hand them the offer + done-for-you collateral** (review page, swipe kit) — the "good thing"
   for the shelf. They don't have to make anything.
3. **They post** their affiliate link (or yours, split via side-agreement); Skool fulfils + pays.
   Faceless, repeatable.

## What this means for the existing build
- **Prospect list:** currently pure-SEO. To match this model it should **expand into adjacent
  money/marketing/agency/MMO/AI niches.** (Next task — pending Shane's go-ahead on breadth.)
- **Assets:** already aligned — the outreach opens with the empty-shelf angle ("your audience
  trusts you and you have no recurring offer for them; I have one, already built"). Review page +
  swipe kit are the collateral. No ebook assets needed.
- **Daily routine:** unchanged — it *is* the automated partnership machine.
- **Models:** unchanged — Rainmakers commission economics still hold (`revenue-model.md`,
  `blended-scenario.md`).

## Honest note on the income figures in the video
The examples in the video ($11K/mo from one ebook, $10K/mo from four deals) are the sales pitch
for a $1,995 course — treat them as marketing, not benchmarks. Our own numbers live in the model
