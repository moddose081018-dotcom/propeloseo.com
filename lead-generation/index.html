LinkedIn Growth Playbook

LinkedIn is the 11th most visited domain from search engines in the US. Posts are ranking on Google. Here's how to capitalize.

Post 3-5x Per Day

Dead accounts getting 12,000 impressions in week 1 just from posting 5x/day. Even 1x/day gets 80,000+ impressions weekly. LinkedIn is rewarding consistent posters heavily right now.

Cross-Post From Other Platforms

Copy your X/Twitter posts directly to LinkedIn. Copy your Instagram captions. Repurpose TikTok scripts as text posts. 5 posts in 30 minutes is achievable when cross-posting.

Write Like a Human, Not a Corporation

Stop using "I'm thrilled to announce" and "I'm proud to share." Write like you're texting a friend. Be authentic, uncensored, direct. This stands out because 90% of LinkedIn content is corporate fluff.

Links in Comments, Not Posts

Posts with links get suppressed in reach. Put your link in the first comment. "Link in the comments" in your post body. This is critical for driving traffic to your site.

Post to Personal Profile, Not Company Page

Manual posting to personal LinkedIn gets dramatically more engagement than automated posts to company pages. The algorithm favors personal accounts.

Don't Stop Posting (Even 48 Hours)

If you stop posting for even 48 hours, your views drop off a cliff. Momentum is everything. Build the habit and don't break the chain.

Proven Content Angles

Angles that consistently attract your ICP:

Beginner-Friendly How-Tos

Posts targeting people setting up their first tank. Use phrases like "no experience needed" and "step-by-step." These attract buyers 3x more than advanced content.

Data-Backed Comparisons

Real test results comparing popular filters. Include specific numbers (flow rate, noise dB, clarity %). Buyers engage heavily with verifiable data.

Money-Saving Tips

"I tested 5 filters under $30" or "Stop wasting money on filters that break." Price-conscious buyers respond strongly to value positioning.

Angles to Avoid

Flexing Business Metrics

"How I grew my review site to 100K visitors" - attracts competitors and peers, NOT buyers. Cut this from your content mix.

Overly Technical Jargon

Deep-dive chemistry posts about nitrogen cycles alienate beginners. Keep it accessible.
