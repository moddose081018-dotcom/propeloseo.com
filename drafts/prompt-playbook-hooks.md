# Opening hooks: AI SEO Prompt Playbook

Ten openings to test on the same sales-page body (`prompt-playbook-sales-page.md`, from "January was the best month…" onward) and as TikTok first lines. Revised 2026-10-06 after the red-team fact-check; facts match the live sales page.

| # | Pattern borrowed | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Odd, specific mechanism (the "half teaspoon" move) | The obvious fix for this client was more content. |
| 2 | Audience call-out + warning | If your traffic dropped after Google's May core update, don't write another word until you've read your own data. |
| 3 | Hidden-cause reveal | A page with over 4,000 impressions had been switched off. |
| 4 | Number contrast | Two groups of searches. Same impressions. One got over 1,000 clicks. The other got about 15. |
| 5 | "It's not what you think" myth-bust | A traffic drop isn't always a content problem. |
| 6 | Shock statistic | Over 700 generic keywords. Together they brought under 1% of the site's clicks. |
| 7 | "If you're like me" empathy | After a traffic drop, most of us open a blank doc and start rewriting. |
| 8 | Missed-opportunity frame | The pages answering "is this site legit?" had fewer than 5 internal links. Boilerplate pages on the same site had over 250. |
| 9 | Debunk the scary number | Part of this site's drop in impressions looked like bots, not people. In one day, impressions fell by over 90%. |
| 10 | Light villain (industry advice) | The usual advice after a drop is publish more. |

## Test plan
- TikTok: one hook per video, same body. Change only the hook (one variable per test, per the TikTok handover).
- Sales page: rotate the headline only. Judge on click-through to checkout, not views.
- Winner rule: same as the TikTok handover, at least 1.5x the baseline before scaling.
