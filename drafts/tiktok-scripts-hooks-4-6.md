# TikTok scripts: hooks 4 and 6

Built to the TikTok handover spec: 35–42 words, 4–6 lines, ≤15 words per line, hook first, at least one first-person line. **Source:** every line is taken verbatim or trimmed from `prompt-playbook-sales-page.md` (no words added). Shoot both with the same setting and delivery so the hook is the only variable.

## Production notes
- **Owner footage first** (handover lesson 2): you on camera, phone at arm's length, one take. Then `isAiGenerated: false`. If rendered with the AI likeness, `true`.
- **No CTA line in the VO** yet: the bio link and price aren't decided. Add "Link in bio" to the caption once the sales page is live.
- **Compliance:** no ranking guarantees, no client name, no niche. The client is anonymised.
- **Test:** post both, read views at 24 h, 72 h and 7 days. Winner needs ≥200 views and ≥1.5x baseline before the other hooks get built.
