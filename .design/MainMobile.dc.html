SEO and GEO

AI SEO and traditional SEO are not the same job

Traditional SEO

Earns a blue link in a list of search results, at a position stable enough to track. Measured against a search engine results page.

AI SEO, or GEO

Earns a mention inside the answer, where there may be no list at all. Measured by asking AI search engines real buyer questions.

The overlap is real: AI engines still lean on crawlability, structure and credible citations, so technical SEO is the floor. Nothing here asks you to replace traditional SEO — both scoreboards run together.

AI SEO services: FAQ

What is AI SEO?

Getting a business surfaced by AI search engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, rather than only in the traditional list of search results. It combines classic technical SEO with measurement of what AI models actually say.

Does AI SEO replace traditional SEO?

No. AI systems still rely on crawlable, well-structured, credible pages. AI SEO adds a second scoreboard on top. Any service telling you to replace traditional SEO outright is selling you a story.

What is generative engine optimization?

GEO is another name for the same discipline, sometimes also called answer engine optimization. What matters is whether a provider measures AI visibility or only claims to.

Which AI platforms do you track?

ChatGPT, Perplexity AI, Google Gemini and Google AI Overviews as standard, with Microsoft Bing chat and AI Mode in the audit.

What exactly is a Growth Pack?

One clearly scoped, publish-ready deliverable built from your live SEO data — a content brief, a technical fix spec, an on-page optimisation, or a competitive gap analysis.

Do you implement changes every month?

Month one includes one eligible fix, implemented. From month two, Growth Packs are publish-ready deliverables ready for you or your developer.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. No minimum commitment, no cancellation friction. You keep everything delivered and have 30 days to redeem remaining credits.

Do you guarantee rankings or traffic?

No. We guarantee delivery of your Growth Pack on time and as specified. The 90-Day Progress Report shows what can and cannot reasonably be attributed to the work.
