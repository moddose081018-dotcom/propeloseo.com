Why now

Why AI SEO matters now

The search experience has changed shape. A growing share of questions are answered inside an AI overview or a chat reply, so the click that used to arrive from a blue link now arrives, if at all, as AI referral traffic from a citation. Buyers who rely on AI assistants to shortlist vendors may never see a results page at all.

That makes AI search optimization a distribution question, not a vanity metric. If AI platforms consistently name three competitors and not you, you are absent from the shortlist before any comparison starts. Businesses that need AI SEO usually discover it late, after traffic has already drifted.

Generative search does not run on a separate internet. It reads the same pages, so SEO best practices still apply — but what gets rewarded shifts toward clarity, structure and citable specifics rather than keyword density.

SEO and GEO

AI SEO and traditional SEO are not the same job

They overlap heavily, but they are not interchangeable — and a service that only does one leaves the other unmanaged.

Traditional SEO

Earns a blue link in a list of search results.
Position is stable enough to track day to day.
Measured against a search engine results page.

AI SEO, or GEO

Earns a mention inside the answer, where there may be no list at all.
No stable position, so measurement has to be built differently.
Measured by asking AI search engines real buyer questions.

The overlap is real. AI engines still lean on crawlability, clean structure, topical depth and credible citations, so technical SEO and generative engine optimization sit on the same foundation. If your pages are slow, blocked or thin, neither Google nor a large language model can use them. That part has not changed.

Nothing here asks you to replace traditional SEO. Both scoreboards are run together, with SEO strategies designed to serve each at once, and both feed into the same monthly deliverable.

Buyer's guide

How to choose the right AI SEO service

Most AI SEO agencies are selling one of three things: a dashboard, a retainer, or a rebranded content mill. Five questions separate them.

01

Does it ship work, or only reports?

A report you have to act on yourself is a tool, not a service, and should be priced like one. Ask what physically arrives each month and who implements it. Good AI SEO services follow through to something publishable.

02

Does it measure AI search at all?

Many providers added “AI SEO” to a page without adding any measurement of AI Overviews, AI Mode or chat assistants. Ask which AI platforms are tested, how often, and whether the prompt set stays constant between months. Without that, AI search results are anecdote.

03

Is the technical SEO floor covered?

Generative engine optimization services layered on a site AI crawlers cannot reach are wasted spend. Ask whether a technical crawl is included, and what happens when it finds something broken.

04

Is the scope honest about what it excludes?

We do not do link building, pay-per-click, or full-service digital marketing. Those are separate disciplines with separate budgets, and a provider claiming all of them for $97 is not being straight with you.

05

Are the limits written down?

Honest SEO services state what is excluded. Ours are in the terms: no development work, no migrations, no template changes, no high-risk site-wide edits.

Questions

AI SEO services: frequently asked questions

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is the work of getting a business surfaced by AI search engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, rather than only in the traditional list of search results. It combines classic technical SEO with measurement of what AI models actually say when asked a buying question. Some providers call it SEO for AI; the work is the same.

Does AI SEO replace traditional SEO?

No. AI systems still rely on crawlable, well-structured, credible pages, so traditional SEO remains the foundation. AI SEO adds a second scoreboard on top of it. Any service telling you to replace traditional SEO outright is selling you a story.

What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization, or GEO, is another name for the same discipline — sometimes also called answer engine optimization, or AEO. The terminology is still settling. What matters is whether a provider measures AI visibility or only claims to.

Which AI platforms do you track?

ChatGPT, Perplexity AI, Google Gemini and Google AI Overviews as standard, with Microsoft Bing chat and AI Mode included in the audit. Coverage follows where your buyers actually ask, not where the marketing is loudest.

What exactly is a Growth Pack?

A Growth Pack is one clearly scoped, publish-ready deliverable built from your live SEO data. It could be a content brief, a technical fix specification, an on-page optimisation, or a competitive gap analysis — whichever represents your highest-impact opportunity that month. Every pack is evidence-based and personally reviewed before delivery.

What happens in month one?

In month one, we set up your full baseline (technical crawl, keyword tracking, competitor snapshot, and AI visibility check), identify your top three opportunities, and deliver a Quick-Win Pack. This first pack includes one eligible low-risk fix that we implement with your written approval — within five business days of completed onboarding and access.

Do you implement changes every month?

The first month includes one eligible fix, implemented. From month two onward, Growth Packs are publish-ready deliverables — scoped, documented, and ready for you or your developer to implement. Routine implementation is excluded after month one but can be purchased separately.

What counts as an "eligible" Quick-Win fix?

Eligible fixes include metadata changes, broken internal links, basic schema corrections, indexability-setting corrections, and one small on-page improvement. Excluded: development work, template changes, migrations, complex schema, plugin conflicts, and high-risk site-wide changes. These boundaries are defined in the terms so the promise stays honest.

You receive one Growth Credit per month. Each credit redeems for one Growth Pack. You can hold a maximum of three credits at a time, and each credit expires individually 90 days after issue. No. Rankings, traffic and revenue depend on variables outside any SEO provider's control. We guarantee the delivery of your Growth Pack on time and as specified.
