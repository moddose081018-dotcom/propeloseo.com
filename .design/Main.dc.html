Questions

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is a Growth Pack?

A Growth Pack is one clearly scoped, publish-ready deliverable built from your live SEO data. It could be a content brief, a technical fix specification, an on-page optimisation, or a competitive gap analysis — whichever represents your highest-impact opportunity that month. Every pack is evidence-based and personally reviewed before delivery.

What happens in month one?

In month one, we set up your full baseline (technical crawl, keyword tracking, competitor snapshot, and AI visibility check), identify your top three opportunities, and deliver a Quick-Win Pack. This first pack includes one eligible low-risk fix that we implement with your written approval — within five business days of completed onboarding and access.

Do you implement changes every month?

The first month includes one eligible fix, implemented. From month two onward, Growth Packs are publish-ready deliverables — scoped, documented, and ready for you or your developer to implement. Routine implementation is excluded after month one but can be purchased separately.

What counts as an "eligible" Quick-Win fix?

Eligible fixes include metadata changes, broken internal links, basic schema corrections, indexability-setting corrections, and one small on-page improvement. Excluded: development work, template changes, migrations, complex schema, plugin conflicts, and high-risk site-wide changes. These boundaries are defined in the terms so the promise stays honest.

How do Growth Credits work?

You receive one Growth Credit per month. Each credit redeems for one Growth Pack. You can hold a maximum of three credits at a time, and each credit expires individually 90 days after issue. Normal redemption is one per month; additional redemptions are subject to capacity. If you cancel, you have 30 days to redeem any remaining credits.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. There is no minimum commitment and no cancellation friction. If you cancel, you keep everything delivered to date and have 30 days to redeem any remaining Growth Credits. We earn your continued membership through visible, accumulating value.

Do you guarantee rankings or traffic?

No. Rankings, traffic and revenue depend on variables outside any SEO provider's control. We guarantee the delivery of your Growth Pack on time and as specified. The 90-Day Progress Report shows your baseline, work delivered, observed movement, and — critically — what can and cannot reasonably be attributed to the work.

What about managed SEO?

Managed SEO retainers start at $2,000 per month and are a separate engagement with a separate qualification process. If your business needs full-service implementation beyond Growth Packs, get in touch and we'll discuss whether managed SEO is the right fit.
