# Blended Scenario — Fastest Realistic Path to $20K/mo

Combines all engines from `revenue-model.md` and `paid-advertising-model.md` into one
month-by-month projection, with 6% monthly churn compounding on the recurring base.

**Bottom line up front:** Blended, the base case reaches **~$19K/mo at month 12** and crosses
**$20K early in month 13**. Hitting $20K by **month 6 is not realistic** at base assumptions
(month 6 lands ~$6.9K). The blend's value isn't speed at base — it's **robustness** (four
lines instead of one) and a credible path to $20K by **month 8–9 if the top levers overperform**.

### The Citation Playbook dependency
Without engine 5, month-12 blended MRR drops to **~$16K** ($3K comes from the Playbook). It's
the second-biggest line and the one Shane fully controls and keeps 100% of — so it's the most
reliable lever, but it's currently unmodeled with real numbers. Supplying its price + members
is the single highest-value input to firm up this projection.

## What it would take to hit $20K faster

| Target | What has to happen |
|---|---|
| **$20K by month 6** | Not realistic — needs ~3x base adds AND a scaled Playbook from day 1. This is the all-optimistic stack, not a plan. |
| **$20K by month 8–9** | Achievable if the top levers overperform: churn held to **4%** (not 6%), **8–10 active partners live by month 3** (not 6), and Citation Playbook scaled to ~$4–5K/mo. |
| **$20K by month 12–13** | The base case above. The expected outcome if execution is solid. |

Lower churn is the quietest but strongest lever: dropping 6% → 4% raises steady-state member
lifetime from ~17 to ~25 months, lifting the whole recurring base ~40% over time.

---

## Fastest realistic path (recommended sequence)

1. **Months 1–2 — activate partners.** Highest leverage in the whole model. Get 6–8 genuinely
   bought-in partners live, not 20 lukewarm sign-ups. Front-loading this pulls the entire curve
   left.
2. **Month 3 — layer retargeting.** Once partner content + outreach create a visitor pool,
   retarget review-page visitors (2–5x cold conversion, positive CAC). Don't run cold ads to the
   $289 offer — see paid-advertising-model.md.
3. **Parallel — grow Citation Playbook.** 100% margin, compounds, and feeds Rainmakers
   graduates. Worth ~$3K/mo of the month-12 total and fully in Shane's control.
4. **Months 4–6 — close 2 additional partnership deals.** Diversifies away from a single
   third-party offer and adds a second recurring line.
5. **Throughout — guard churn.** Pick partners whose audiences retain; churn compounds against
   you every month.

**Net:** $20K/mo is a **month 11–13 target at base**, compressible to **month 8–9** if partner
activation is fast, churn stays low, and the Playbook scales. The 6-month goal in the original
brief is the optimistic ceiling, not the expected path.
