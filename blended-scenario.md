# Blended Scenario — Path to $20K/mo (94-prospect pool, with costs)

Combines all engines from `revenue-model.md` and `paid-advertising-model.md` into one
month-by-month **net P&L** (revenue minus costs), with 6% monthly churn compounding on the
recurring base. Updated for the expanded **94-prospect** list and now including the cost side.

**Bottom line up front:** Base case reaches **~$21.6K/mo gross revenue at month 12**, and
**net profit crosses $20K/mo around month 12**. The business is **cash-positive from month 2** —
the only loss month is month 1 (–$128), because Shane's $179/mo Rainmakers membership is paid
before referrals kick in. Cumulative year-1 net ≈ **~$107.8K** on ~$8.4K of cash costs. Month 6
nets ~$7.2K. The original 6-month goal remains the optimistic ceiling; month 12 is the expected path.

---

## What the bigger pool changes (and what it doesn't)
The pool nearly doubled (50 → 94) and half now have verified sizes, so partner *selection* is
better and there are more shots on goal. But partner **activation is bounded by Shane's time and
the ≤10 drafts/day cap, not by prospect count** — so the ramp improves ~15–20%, not 2x. Modeled
active partners rise from ~6 to ~8 over 6 months.

## The Citation Playbook dependency
Engine 5 uses **illustrative** numbers (60 members × $50 by month 12 = ~$3K/mo, on a $99/mo Skool
cost). Strip it out and month-12 net drops ~$2.9K to ~**$17.8K**. It's the line Shane fully
controls and keeps 100% of — supplying its real price + member count is the single highest-value
input to firm up this projection.

## What it takes to hit $20K faster
| Target | What has to happen |
|---|---|
| **$20K net by month 6** | Not realistic — needs ~3x base adds AND a scaled Playbook from day 1. |
| **$20K net by month 8–9** | Churn held to **4%** (not 6%), **10+ active partners live by month 3**, Playbook at ~$4–5K/mo. |
| **$20K net by month 12** | The base case above — expected outcome with solid execution. |

Lowering churn 6% → 4% raises member lifetime ~17 → ~25 months and lifts the whole recurring base
~40% over time — the quietest, strongest lever, and it costs nothing.

## Fastest realistic path
1. **Months 1–2 — activate partners** (highest leverage). 8+ genuinely bought-in partners from
   the 94-pool, best fit-scores first.
2. **Month 3 — layer retargeting** on the visitor pool partners create (positive CAC; not cold ads).
3. **Parallel — grow Citation Playbook** (100% margin, compounds, feeds Rainmakers graduates).
4. **Months 4–6 — close 2 additional partnership deals.**
5. **Throughout — guard churn.**
