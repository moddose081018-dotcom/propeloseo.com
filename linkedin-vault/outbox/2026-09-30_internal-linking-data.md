53% of your pages probably have 3 or fewer internal links pointing to them. Here's what that's costing you.

**Slide 1 — Title**
What 23 Million Internal Links Tell Us About Rankings
(Zyppy study: 1,800 sites, 520K URLs, cross-referenced with Search Console clicks)

**Slide 2 — The Sweet Spot**
Pages with 40–44 internal links earn roughly 4x more clicks than pages with fewer than 5.

After 45–50, the benefit reverses and traffic declines.

There's a ceiling. Most sites aren't even close to it.

**Slide 3 — Anchor Text Is Not an Afterthought**
Pages with at least one exact-match anchor text got 5x more traffic than pages without.

Most teams treat internal anchor text as random. It shouldn't be.

**Slide 4 — The URL Anchor Trick**
Pages with URL anchors from internal links saw ~50% more traffic than pages without.

Small technical detail. Disproportionate impact.

**Slide 5 — The Under-Linked Problem**
53% of URLs in the study had 3 or fewer internal links.

More than half of most sites are essentially orphaned from their own link graph.

**Slide 6 — The 82% Gap**
82% of internal linking opportunities are missed by most sites (InLinks data across thousands of domains).

This is probably the highest-ROI SEO task most teams aren't doing.

**Slide 7 — What the Before/After Looks Like**
One case study: 180 strategic internal links added over 2 weeks → 220% organic traffic increase in 4 months + 8 featured snippets captured.

Not a redesign. Not new content. Just connecting what already existed.

**Slide 8 — CTA**
What does your internal linking audit look like? Have you found a similar sweet spot in your data?
