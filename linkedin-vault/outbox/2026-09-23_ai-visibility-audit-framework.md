The 5-step AI visibility audit we run on every new client.

Most have never checked if ChatGPT even mentions their brand.

**Slide 1 — Title**
The 5-Step AI Visibility Audit
(What we run on every new client before touching a single ranking)

**Slide 2 — Step 1: Build Your Prompt Set**
Skip keyword tools. Poll your sales team for the questions buyers actually ask.

Unbranded queries ("best [category] for [use case]"), comparison prompts ("[you] vs [competitor]"), and solution-aware questions ("how to fix [problem]").

These are the prompts AI engines answer. If you're not in those answers, you're invisible.

**Slide 3 — Step 2: Test Across 5 Platforms**
ChatGPT. Perplexity. Gemini. Google AI Overviews. Claude.

Logged out. Incognito. No personalization noise.

Run every prompt on every platform. The results will shock you — only 11% of domains get cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity. They pull from completely different source pools.

**Slide 4 — Step 3: Track 3 Outcomes**
For each prompt, record:
- Mentions (brand named in answer text)
- Citations (brand in source references)
- Click-throughs (links pointing to your site)

These are three different metrics. A mention without a citation is awareness. A citation without a click-through is wasted.

**Slide 5 — Step 4: Build a Citation Source Ledger**
List every domain the AI cited in your category. Sort by frequency.

The top 20 cited domains explain most of what the machine believes about your space. If you're not in that top 20, you know exactly who to study.

**Slide 6 — Step 5: Measure the Gap**
Compare your AI citation share vs. your Google rankings.

73% of brands ranking on page one have zero AI mentions. Brands that do get cited see a 38% lift in organic clicks and 23x higher conversion probability from AI referral traffic.

The gap between "ranking well" and "cited by AI" is your opportunity.

**Slide 7 — What We Found**
We ran this on 14 client sites last quarter.

Only 2 were being cited by ChatGPT.

The other 12 had zero visibility in AI answers despite ranking on page one for their core terms.
