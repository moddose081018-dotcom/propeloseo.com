Surfer analyzed 5 million AI citations. Domain authority predicted almost none of them.

They pulled three months of data — 20,000 prompts, 5 million citation URLs across AI Mode, AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity.

The finding: DA correlates negatively with AI citation frequency. But barely. Close enough to zero to be noise.

That's not a weak correlation. It's no correlation.

Meanwhile, Ahrefs' 75,000-brand analysis found brand mentions correlate 3x more strongly with AI visibility than backlinks (0.664 vs 0.218).

E-E-A-T signals? r=0.81.

So what actually drives AI citations:

- Content freshness
- Answer-ready depth (does your page directly answer the question?)
- Quotable evidence density (statistics, cited sources, original data)
- Brand mentions across the web

What doesn't drive them: the metric most SEOs check first.

We stopped reporting DA to clients three months ago. Replaced it with AI citation share by platform. The conversations got more productive immediately.

The honest constraint: DA still matters for traditional Google rankings. It's not dead. But if you're leading your strategy with it in 2026, you're optimizing for one game while a second game is already pulling your clients' traffic.

If you're still leading client reports with DA, what's the argument for keeping it? Genuinely curious — what am I missing?
