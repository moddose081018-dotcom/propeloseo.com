We built a client's entire strategy around long-tail keywords. Then AI Overviews showed up on 73% of them.

B2B SaaS client in the compliance space. We'd mapped 200+ long-tail keywords, built content around every one. Rankings were strong. Traffic was growing.

Then we pulled the AI Overview data last month. 34 of their top 50 long-tail pages now had an AI Overview sitting above them. That's the before picture.

For years, the playbook was simple: find long-tail keywords with low competition, answer the query, rank, collect traffic. That playbook assumed you'd get the click.

AI Overviews changed the math. 7-word queries trigger them at 7x the rate of shorter ones. 8+ word queries see overviews 73% more frequently.

The exact territory we'd built the strategy on was now where AI Overviews were most aggressive.

60% of Google searches now end without a click. For informational long-tail, it's worse.

But here's what saved the engagement: AI Overviews pull citations from far deeper than traditional ranking ever allowed. Citations from positions 21–30 increased by 400%.

Pages that never cracked the top 10 are showing up in AI-generated answers. That changes who gets traffic — and it's not who you'd expect.

The new long-tail playbook isn't about ranking first. It's about being citable.

Different content structure. Different signals. Different goal entirely.

What we shifted: less "answer the query perfectly in 2,000 words" and more "be the source with unique data or first-hand experience that AI wants to reference."

Have you had to rebuild a long-tail strategy around AI Overviews yet? What did you change first?
