A client asked why their traffic dropped 20% while rankings stayed flat.

I didn't have a good answer. Because our dashboard didn't track where the clicks were actually going.

Turns out AI Overviews were answering their top queries directly. Users got what they needed without clicking through. Rankings held. Traffic left.

That question broke our entire reporting model.

The old stack: Search Console, rank tracker, backlink monitor, GA4. Standard stuff that's worked since 2018.

What we added:

- AI citation tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and AI Overviews
- Share of voice in AI answers vs. competitors
- Citation source ledger — which of our pages AI engines actually reference
- AI Overview cannibalization report — queries with high impressions, low CTR, confirmed AI Overview presence

The honest constraint: the tooling is still rough. We're stitching together 3-4 platforms. There's no single dashboard that does all of this yet.

But the conversations with clients changed overnight.

Instead of "your rankings are stable" (while traffic quietly drops), we can show exactly where attention moved and what to do about it.

58% of searches now result in zero clicks. If your reports don't explain where those non-clicks went, you're reporting on half the picture.

What does your 2026 reporting stack look like? Still ranking-first, or have you added AI visibility?
