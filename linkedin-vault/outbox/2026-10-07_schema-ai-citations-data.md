Ahrefs tested 1,885 pages adding schema markup. AI citations moved +2.2%. That's statistically zero.

**Slide 1 — Title**
Schema Markup & AI Citations: Correlation Is Not Causation
(Ahrefs study: 1,885 pages tracked, 4,000 controls, 6M URLs analyzed)

**Slide 2 — The Correlation**
Pages cited by AI are 3x more likely to include JSON-LD schema markup.

Sounds like a slam dunk for adding schema to everything.

**Slide 3 — The Experiment**
Ahrefs tracked 1,885 pages that added JSON-LD between Aug 2025 and Mar 2026. Matched against 4,000 control pages. Measured citation changes across Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, and ChatGPT.

**Slide 4 — The Results**
ChatGPT citations: +2.2% (statistically indistinguishable from zero)
Google AI Mode: +2.4% (also zero)
Google AI Overviews: -4.6% (slight decline)

Adding schema moved nothing.

**Slide 5 — Why the Disconnect**
Schema lives on better-maintained, more technically sophisticated sites. Those sites also publish stronger content, build more authority, earn more links.

Schema isn't driving citations. It's just correlated with the things that do.

**Slide 6 — The FAQPage Wrinkle**
A separate analysis of 127K AI answer panels found FAQPage schema on 58.3% of cited sources — 3.24x more likely to be cited.

Then Google killed FAQ rich results in May 2026. The schema that correlated most with AI citations lost its primary search function.

**Slide 7 — What Actually Gets Cited**
Across 6M URLs, the sites earning AI citations win on content depth, topical authority, and link signals. Not structured data alone.

Schema without substance is markup decorating mediocrity.

**Slide 8 — CTA**
Are you investing in schema for AI visibility? What's actually moved the needle for citations on your sites?

## First comment (post within 5 minutes)
Full study from Ahrefs — link below. Their methodology matters here: they used a matched difference-in-differences design to isolate the causal effect of adding schema, rather than just observing correlation. Most of the "schema boosts AI citations" claims are correlation studies. This one tested causation. Important distinction. That said, schema is still worth implementing for crawlability, rich snippets where they still exist, and general technical hygiene. Just don't expect it to unlock AI citations on its own.
