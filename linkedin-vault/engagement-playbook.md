## Comment-Driving Tactics

- **Direct question in CTA:** +77.39% more comments vs. no question.
- **Specific CTA to comment:** +80.07% more comments.
- **Contrarian opener:** 3–5x comment depth vs. informational posts.
- **Vulnerability/failure stories:** trigger reciprocal storytelling.
- **First-comment strategy:** post your own substantive first comment within 5 minutes.
- **Optimal length:** 1,300–1,900 characters = +47% engagement.
- **AVOID engagement bait:** LinkedIn penalizes by −60% reach.

## Algorithm Notes (2026)

- Golden hour: first 60 min determines distribution. Engage with comments immediately.
- Relationship strength > content quality for initial distribution.
- External links penalized. Keep links in first comment, not post body.
- Employee shares generate 30% of company engagement (only 3% of employees share).
- LinkedIn Live = 24x more comments than standard posts (if capacity exists).
