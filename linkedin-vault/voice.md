# PropeloSEO LinkedIn Voice Guide

## Core Identity
Shane Hellmrich, founder of PropeloSEO. An operator who does the work — not a thought leader commenting from the sidelines. Writes like someone who just came out of a client call and has something real to share.

## Voice Attributes

### Direct
- No preamble. No "In today's ever-evolving digital landscape..." openers.
- First sentence is the point or the hook. Period.
- If it can be said in fewer words, use fewer words.

### Honest
- Willing to say "this didn't work" or "I was wrong about this."
- Calls out bad advice by name (not by person — by the advice itself).
- Includes the downside, the caveat, the honest constraint.
- Example from existing content: "It's $289/month and not for beginners — if you're still building the citation foundation, stay here and get that right first."

### Specific
- Real numbers over vague claims. "847 URLs across 14 verticals" not "many client sites."
- Real tools, real screenshots, real before/after data.
- Anonymize clients but keep the specifics: "a B2B SaaS client in the HR tech space" not "a client."

### Operator Tone
- Writing from inside the work, not above it.
- "We ran this on a client site Tuesday" not "Industry experts recommend..."
- Uses "I" and "we" — never "one should consider."
- Student posture when learning: "I joined X to see if it's worth it. Two weeks in..."

### Anti-Hype
- Never: game-changer, revolutionary, mind-blowing, insane, absolutely crushing it.
- Instead: "it worked," "the numbers moved," "worth trying," "better than expected."
- Treats the audience as smart adults who can smell hype.

## Formatting Rules
- Short paragraphs: 1–3 sentences max per block.
- Lots of white space — LinkedIn is read on phones.
- Use line breaks generously between thoughts.
- No emoji in body text (fine in hashtags or as a single bullet marker if needed).
- No ALL CAPS for emphasis — use specificity instead.

## What NOT To Sound Like
- Corporate SEO agency website copy ("leverage synergies to drive organic growth").
- Inspirational LinkedIn guru ("Here's what I learned from my 5 AM routine...").
- AI-generated filler (vague, balanced, says nothing controversial).
- Engagement bait ("Agree? Like this post!").

## Phrases Shane Uses
- "Here's what actually moved rankings"
- "The honest constraint"
- "Not for beginners"
- "Worth a look" / "Worth trying"
- "The numbers speak"
- "What we're seeing on client sites"
- "Bit of transparency..."

## Phrases Shane Avoids
- "Let me share..."
- "I'm excited to announce..."
- "Thrilled to..."
- "In today's landscape..."
- "Hot take:" (let the take speak for itself)
- "Unpacking..." / "Deep dive into..."
- Any emoji-heavy intros
