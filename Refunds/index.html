Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

PropeloSEO is committed to fair, transparent refund terms. We earn your continued membership through delivered value, not cancellation friction.

First-month delivery guarantee

Your first Quick-Win Pack — including one eligible fix, implemented — arrives within five business days of completed onboarding, required access and your written approval. If it is late or materially incomplete, your first month is refunded in full.

Growth Membership ($97/mo)

The Growth Membership has no minimum commitment. You may cancel at any time.

  • First month: Covered by the delivery guarantee above. If your first Quick-Win Pack is late or materially incomplete, you receive a full refund of your first month.
  • Ongoing months: Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. No partial-month refunds are issued because you retain access to all membership benefits through the end of the paid period.
  • Growth Credits on cancellation: You have 30 days after cancellation to redeem any remaining Growth Credits. Unredeemed credits expire after the 30-day grace period.
  • Delivered work: Everything delivered to you during your membership is yours to keep, regardless of cancellation.

AI Visibility Audit ($197)

The AI Visibility Audit is a one-time purchase. If the audit is not delivered within the agreed timeframe, you are entitled to a full refund. Once the audit has been delivered, refunds are not available as the work has been completed.

If you purchase the audit and then join the Growth Membership within 30 days, your first membership month is free. This credit is applied automatically and is non-refundable separately.

Managed SEO retainers

Managed SEO retainers ($2,000+/mo) are governed by a separate engagement agreement with its own refund and cancellation terms.

How to request a refund

To request a refund under the delivery guarantee, email shanehellmrich@proton.me within 14 days of the missed delivery date. Refunds are processed via Stripe within 5–10 business days.

What we don't refund

We do not offer refunds based on SEO outcomes (rankings, traffic, revenue). PropeloSEO guarantees the delivery of work as specified; outcomes that depend on variables outside our control are never guaranteed. This is stated clearly in our Terms of Service and on our pricing page.

Questions

If you have questions about this refund policy, contact us at shanehellmrich@proton.me.
