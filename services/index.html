Quick-Win Pack: eligible scope

Your first Growth Pack includes one eligible low-risk fix, implemented with your written approval, within five business days of completed onboarding and access.

Eligible fixes

  • Metadata changes (title tags, meta descriptions)
  • Broken internal link repairs
  • Basic schema corrections
  • Indexability-setting corrections
  • One small on-page improvement

Excluded from eligible scope

  • Development work
  • Template or theme changes
  • Site migrations
  • Complex schema implementations
  • Plugin conflicts
  • High-risk site-wide changes

From month two onward, Growth Packs are publish-ready deliverables. Routine implementation is excluded unless purchased separately.
